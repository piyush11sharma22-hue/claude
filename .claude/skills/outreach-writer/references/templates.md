## Cold Email — Full Sequence

### Touch 1 (Day 0) — Hook + Value + Soft CTA
**Subject:** `{specific, <45 chars}`

> Hi {First},
>
> {One line about a specific signal — funding, a hire, a launch, a post.}
>
> {One line connecting that to a problem their role owns.} We help {similar
> companies} {specific outcome + one proof metric}.
>
> Worth a quick look? Happy to send a 2-min example — no call needed.
>
> {Your name}

### Touch 2 (Day 3–4) — New Angle / Proof
**Subject:** `Re: {same thread}`

> Hi {First},
>
> One more data point: {Similar Co} saw {specific result} in {timeframe} after
> {what they did}. Thought it might be relevant given {their signal}.
>
> Want me to send the breakdown?
>
> {Your name}

### Touch 3 (Day 7–9) — Different Value Angle / Resource
**Subject:** `Re: {same thread}`

> Hi {First},
>
> Not sure if {problem} is a priority right now — if it is, here's a short
> {resource/teardown} we put together for teams like {Company}: {link}.
>
> Either way, happy to be a resource.
>
> {Your name}

### Touch 4 (Day 14) — Break-up
**Subject:** `Re: {same thread}`

> Hi {First},
>
> I'll close the loop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If {outcome} ever
> becomes a priority, just reply and I'll pick things back up.
>
> Thanks for the read,
> {Your name}

## LinkedIn — Connection Note (< 300 chars)
No pitch. Just the hook + a human reason to connect.

> Hi {First} — saw {specific signal about them/Company}. I work with {similar
> people} on {topic} and would love to follow your work. Mind connecting?

## LinkedIn — First DM (after they accept)

> Thanks for connecting, {First}! Genuinely curious — how are you handling
> {specific workflow} at {Company} these days? We've been solving this for a
> few teams your size and I'd happily share what's working.

## LinkedIn — Follow-up DM

> No worries if it's not a priority — just wanted to share this quick example
> of {result} at {Similar Co}: {link}. Thought it might be relevant to {signal}.

---

## Instagram / X DM (1–3 sentences, casual)
Reference their content. Zero corporate tone.

> Hey {First}! Loved your {specific post/reel/video} on {topic} — the part
> about {detail} really landed. I build {thing} for people doing exactly this;
> mind if I send you a quick idea?

## Instagram / X DM — Follow-up

> No pressure at all — here's the thing I mentioned in case it's useful:
> {link}. Keep up the great content 🙌

## CSV Export Schema
When exporting a batch for a CRM or sequencing tool:

```csv
name,email,linkedin_url,company,role,personalization_hook,angle,subject,touch_1,touch_2,touch_3,touch_4
```

Compatible with import into HubSpot, Apollo, Instantly, Lemlist, Smartlead,
and most CRMs (map columns during import).

## Personalization Signal Bank
Real signals to hunt for and build the hook around (never invent these):

- Recent funding round / new investor
- New job posting (esp. multiple for the same team = a scaling pain)
- A product launch or major release
- A leadership hire or role change
- A podcast, conference talk, webinar, or interview they did
- A LinkedIn/X post they wrote (quote a specific line)
- Tech-stack signals (job descriptions, GitHub, BuiltWith, Stack Share)
- Company news / press / awards
- Expansion into a new market or office
- A public pain point (support forum, review site, changelog gap)
- Mutual connection, alumni, community, or investor overlap
